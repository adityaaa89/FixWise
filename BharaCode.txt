1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ELEVATOR PITCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FixWise is an intelligent, deterministic financial decision engine and multimodal
diagnostic system for household appliances across India. 

When a major home appliance (washing machine, air conditioner, refrigerator, water
heater, water purifier) breaks down, consumers face an anxious, opaque dilemma:
"Should I pay a technician ₹4,500 to repair my 5-year-old appliance, or spend ₹30,000
on a new 5-star inverter model?"

FixWise eliminates this guesswork by pairing Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision OCR
and Adaption AI Labs diagnostic triage with a 100% deterministic mathematical 
lifecycle amortization engine. It evaluates technician quotes against genuine OEM 
parts benchmarks, factors in repeat breakdown risks and Bureau of Energy Efficiency 
(BEE) power tariffs, and delivers an objective "REPAIR" or "REPLACE" verdict with the 
exact tipping point required to flip the decision.


2. THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM IN INDIA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Over 350 million household appliances power Indian homes. Every year, tens of millions 
experience breakdowns, leading to significant consumer frustration:

A) The Sunk-Cost Fallacy & Repeat Failure Trap:
   Consumers frequently sink ₹3,000 - ₹6,000 into repairing aging appliances (6+ years old),
   only for another critical component (compressor, main PCB controller, drum bearings)
   to fail 4 months later. Repeat breakdown rates on late-lifecycle appliances exceed 20-30%.

B) Information Asymmetry & Overcharging:
   Local unorganized technicians routinely inflate repair quotes by 100-300% or misdiagnose
   cheap faults (e.g., a ₹400 microswitch or ₹650 inlet valve) as catastrophic failures
   (e.g., "Full PCB board burnt, ₹6,000").

C) Hidden Energy Costs (The Energy Star Reality):
   An old 10-year-old non-inverter air conditioner or refrigerator consumes ₹8,000 to 
   ₹12,000 more electricity every year compared to a modern BEE 5-star inverter unit. 
   Homeowners focus solely on the upfront repair quote without realizing that keeping 
   an inefficient appliance costs them far more in utility bills.

D) India's E-Waste Crisis:
   Discarding appliances prematurely when a simple ₹800 DIY fix or part replacement 
   would give them 4 more years of life accelerates the mounting electronic waste crisis.


3. THE FIXWISE SOLUTION & HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FixWise combines a guided 3-step interface with a rigorous, transparent pipeline:

STEP 1: Plain-Language Symptom & Profile Intake
   - Users select appliance category (Washing Machine, AC, Refrigerator, RO, Geyser, Microwave).
   - Enter their brand, appliance age (years), and describe symptoms in everyday Indian phrasing
     (e.g., "machine beeps continuously with dE error", "AC outdoor unit cuts off after 10 mins",
     "geyser switch on karte hi 32A MCB trip ho jati hai").

STEP 2: Financial Baseline & Technician Quote
   - FixWise pre-fills real-world Indian retail market benchmarks for new replacement units
     and fair OEM part price bands.
   - If the user has received an estimate from a technician, they enter the exact quote (Q).

STEP 3 (Optional): Multimodal Rating Plate OCR
   - Users drop a photo of their model sticker / rating plate label.
   - Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Vision automatically extracts the exact model number, serial
     number, voltage, frequency, and wattage, eliminating technical data entry errors.

STEP 4: Dual-Engine Evaluation
   - AI Diagnostic Engine: Queries Adaption AI Labs triage models to rank probable component
     defects (door lock microswitch, drain pump, PCB inverter, fan motor) with DIY feasibility flags.
   - Deterministic Math Engine: Python cost engine calculates annualized lifecycle outlays
     for both options and identifies the exact sensitivity tipping point.


4. THE DETERMINISTIC MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A core architectural principle of FixWise is that THE LLM NEVER DOES ARITHMETIC.
All calculations are executed deterministically in Python (backend/cost_engine.py).

A) Total & Annualized Repair Cost:
   C_repair = Q + (tr * v) + (p * Q) + (E_old * L_r)
   Annualized Repair Outlay = C_repair / L_r = [ Q*(1 + p) + (tr * v) ] / L_r + E_old

   Where:
   - Q     = Technician repair quote or OEM part replacement cost
   - p     = Probability of repeat breakdown within extended lifespan (default: 15-20%)
   - tr    = Time/hassle hours lost by owner dealing with technician & waiting (default: 4.0 hrs)
   - v     = Monetary value of consumer's personal time (default: ₹350/hour)
   - E_old = Annual operating & electricity cost of the current old unit (BEE tariff: ₹8.50/unit)
   - L_r   = Expected extended lifespan post-repair (typically 2 - 4 years)

B) Total & Annualized Replace Cost:
   C_replace = P + D - S + (tn * v) + (E_new * L_n)
   Annualized Replace Outlay = C_replace / L_n = [ P + D - S + (tn * v) ] / L_n + E_new

   Where:
   - P     = Purchase price of a modern 4/5-star inverter replacement model
   - D     = Delivery, professional installation, and piping fee (₹800 standard)
   - S     = Scrap, trade-in, or old-unit exchange credit (₹1,500 standard)
   - tn    = Time hours spent researching, ordering, and receiving new unit (5.0 hrs)
   - E_new = Annual electricity cost of modern 5-star inverter model
   - L_n   = Expected lifespan of brand new replacement appliance (8 - 12 years)

C) Objective Decision Rule:
   - If Annualized Repair Outlay < Annualized Replace Outlay: VERDICT = "REPAIR"
   - Otherwise: VERDICT = "REPLACE"

D) Exact Tipping Point (Flip Assumption):
   The sensitivity solver calculates the exact boundary condition where the decision flips:
   - Q_flip: The maximum repair quote before replacement becomes cheaper.
   - L_r_flip: The minimum lifespan the repaired appliance must survive to justify the fix.
   - P_flip: The discount price at which buying new becomes the smarter choice.
